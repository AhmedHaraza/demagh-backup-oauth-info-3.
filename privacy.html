Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

About the Application

Demagh Mobarmeg Backup is a private backup utility used by the administrator of the Demagh Mobarmeg educational platform.

Data We Process

Backups may contain student account information, educational progress, examination records, and study materials.

Google Drive Access

The application uses Google Drive to store and retrieve backup files. It requests access to files created or used by the application.

Data Security

Backup files are encrypted locally using rclone crypt before being uploaded to Google Drive.

Data Sharing

The backup utility is intended for private administrative use. It does not intentionally share backup contents with other users.

Data Retention

Backup files are retained until they are deleted by the administrator.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact: demaghmobarmeg@gmail.com
